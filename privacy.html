Catching Blocks - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Catching Blocks ("the game") is made by Narek Sargsyan. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your progress (scores, coins, the characters, platforms and maps you own, and your sound settings) is saved in a small file on your own device. It never leaves your device and is deleted if you delete the game.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or personal details are asked for.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as the LARPER bundle) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if ads are added), this page will be updated and the date above changed.
