Block Catch! - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Block Catch! ("the game") is made by Narek Sargsyan. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: the developer does not collect any personal information. The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics (Google Firebase) so the game can be improved, and it shows optional video ads from Google, which collect some device information to show and measure them (see "Analytics" and "Ads" below).

What the game stores

Your progress (scores, coins, the characters, platforms and maps you own, and your sound settings) is saved in a small file on your own device. If iCloud is turned on, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account so your progress comes back if you reinstall the game; the developer cannot see it.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or personal details are asked for.
  • No name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location is collected.
  • No advertising identifier is used for analytics.

Analytics

To learn how the game is played and make it better (for example: how long games last, which stage was reached, what was bought in the store, whether an ad was watched), the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase, together with a random app-install ID, device and app information (model, iOS version, app version, country). It is not linked to your name or identity and is not used to track you across other apps. The same service (Firebase Remote Config) lets the developer adjust game settings, such as difficulty, without an app update, and try different settings with different groups of players. See Firebase privacy.

Ad measurement

The developer may advertise the game on other apps and websites. To learn which of those ads led to an install, the game uses AppsFlyer, which receives the install, the gameplay events above, purchases made in the game, device and app information and IP address, and your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when the game asks. AppsFlyer shares the result with the ad network that showed the ad (for example AppLovin). See AppsFlyer's privacy policy.

Ads

After a game you can choose to watch a short video ad to continue or to get bonus coins. Ads are only shown when you tap a "WATCH AD" button. They are provided by Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect: your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the game asks), IP address and approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this to show ads that fit your interests and may combine it with information from other apps and websites.

  • When you first open the game, iPhone asks whether to allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still work but are not personalised with your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In regions where the law requires it (for example the EU and UK), Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ads are loaded.
  • How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as the LARPER bundle) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
