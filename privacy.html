Block Catch! - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Block Catch! ("the game") is made by Narek Sargsyan. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: the developer does not collect any personal information. The game shows optional video ads from Google, and Google collects some device information to show and measure them (see "Ads" below).

What the game stores

Your progress (scores, coins, the characters, platforms and maps you own, and your sound settings) is saved in a small file on your own device. It never leaves your device and is deleted if you delete the game.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or personal details are asked for.
  • No analytics of its own. No data is sent to the developer.

Ads

After a game you can choose to watch a short video ad to continue or to get bonus coins. Ads are only shown when you tap a "WATCH AD" button. They are provided by Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect: your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the game asks), IP address and approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this to show ads that fit your interests and may combine it with information from other apps and websites.

  • When you first open the game, iPhone asks whether to allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still work but are not personalised with your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In regions where the law requires it (for example the EU and UK), Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ads are loaded.
  • How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as the LARPER bundle) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
