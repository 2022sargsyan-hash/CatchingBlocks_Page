Block Catch! - Support

Need help? Email 2022sargsyan@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

Drag anywhere to move the tray and catch the falling blocks. Catches build your combo; missing a block costs a life. Don't catch bombs. Coins from each game buy characters and platforms in the Store.

I bought something but don't have it

Open the Store and tap RESTORE PURCHASES (top right). Sign in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Your purchases come back for free.

New phone or reinstalled the game

Progress is saved on the device only, so scores and coins start over on a new phone. Purchases made with real money can always be restored with RESTORE PURCHASES.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
